Frequently asked questions

Select a question to see the answer.

What currency is used?

Taniti uses the U.S. dollar. Many businesses also accept euros and yen, several banks exchange currency, and major credit cards are widely accepted.

What kind of power outlets are there?

Outlets are 120 volts, the same as in the United States. Visitors from other regions may need an adapter.

Do people speak English?

Many younger Tanitians speak fluent English. Very little English is spoken in rural areas, especially by older residents.

Are attractions open on holidays?

Taniti has many national holidays. Many attractions and restaurants close on those days, so plan ahead and check before you book.

Is Taniti safe?

Violent crime is very rare. As tourism grows there are more reports of pickpocketing and petty theft, so keep your valuables secure.

Where can I get medical care?

Taniti has one hospital, with many multilingual staff, and several clinics.

What is the drinking age?

The drinking age on Taniti is 18. Alcohol is not served or sold between midnight and 9:00 a.m.
