Transportation

Getting to Taniti

By air

Almost all visitors fly into Taniti's airport, which serves small jets and propeller planes. An expansion will let larger jets land within the next few years.

By cruise ship

A small cruise ship docks in Yellow Leaf Bay one night each week, steps from Taniti City's beaches.

Getting around the island

City bus

Taniti City · 5 a.m. to 11 p.m. daily

Public buses cover Taniti City every day of the week.

Private bus

Rest of the island

Private bus companies serve areas outside Taniti City.

Taxi

Taniti City

Taxis are available throughout Taniti City.

Rental car

Near the airport

Rent a car from the local agency near the airport.

Bike rental

Several vendors

Bikes and helmets are available to rent. Helmets are required by law.

Walking

Taniti City and Merriton Landing

Taniti City is flat and very walkable, and Merriton Landing is easy to explore on foot.
